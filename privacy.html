Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

The Sunshine Fabrics Stock Register is a stock-keeping program for fabric quality, colour and quantity. It runs entirely in your web browser. There is no server behind it, and the people who make it cannot see what you record in it.

In short: your stock register is kept on your own device, and — only if you switch Google Drive sync on — as one file in your own Google Drive. It is never sent to us or to anybody else.

What the program stores, and where

  • On your device. Everything you enter — entries, quantities, customers, suppliers, slip numbers and settings — is saved in your browser's own storage on the device you typed it on. It stays there until you clear it.
  • In your Google Drive, only if you connect it. Google Drive sync is off until you press Connect Google Drive. Once connected, the register is also kept as a single file in your Google Drive account, so your other devices can read it. You own that file.

What the program asks Google for

When you connect Google Drive, the program asks for one permission: drive.file. That permission lets it see and change only the files it created itself. It cannot read, list or touch anything else in your Google Drive — not your documents, not your photos, not your other files.

The program reads your Google account only far enough to sign in and write its own file. It does not read your name, your contacts, your email or your calendar.

What is not collected

  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no advertising cookies.
  • No stock figures, customer names or supplier names are sent to us. We have no server to send them to.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with any third party, because nothing is collected.

Who else is involved

Only Google, and only if you choose to connect Drive — for signing in and for storing your file in your own account. That part is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. The program itself is served as plain files from GitHub Pages, which may record ordinary web-server request logs such as IP addresses, as any website does.

How long it is kept, and how to remove it

  • On the device: clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • In Google Drive: delete the register file from your Drive, or remove the app's access at your Google account permissions page.
  • Stop syncing without deleting: press Disconnect in the Setup tab. The register stays on the device.

Because there is no server, nobody else can delete your data for you — and nobody else can read it either.

Children

This is a tool for running a business. It is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
